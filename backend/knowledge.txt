SECTION 1: OVERVIEW AND MANDATORY DISCLAIMER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Training and Placement Cell facilitates corporate interaction, conducts 
campus recruitment drives, and organizes career skill training programs.

IMPORTANT POLICY DISCLAIMER:
The Placement Cell acts as a facilitator connecting registered students with 
prospective corporate recruiters. The Placement Cell does NOT guarantee job 
offers or predict selection outcomes. Final candidate selection, compensation 
structures, job designations, and joining terms are strictly at the sole discretion 
of the recruiting organization. No candidate is guaranteed placement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: STUDENT ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Minimum Academic Benchmarks:
- Standard Minimum CGPA: Minimum aggregate CGPA of 6.00 across all completed semesters 
  without active academic backlogs, unless a company specifies higher requirements.
- Tier 1 Super Dream Criteria: Typically requires a minimum CGPA of 8.00 or higher, 
  with no historical backlogs.
- Tier 1 Dream Criteria: Typically requires a minimum CGPA of 7.00 or higher.
- Tier 2 Core / Mass Criteria: Typically requires a minimum CGPA of 6.00 or 6.50.
- Class 10 and Class 12 / Diploma Requirements: Many recruiters mandate 60.0% or 65.0% 
  minimum aggregate in 10th and 12th/Diploma examinations.

2.2 Backlog Policy:
- Active Backlogs: Students with active (standing) backlogs at the start of a drive 
  are ineligible for Tier 1 drives. Certain Tier 2 companies may permit candidates with 
  at most [ALLOWED_BACKLOG_COUNT_TIER2: currently 0 to 1 active backlog, subject to 
  specific company criteria].
- Historical Backlogs (Cleared Backlogs): Cleared backlogs are accepted by select 
  recruiters, but top-tier firms mandate an unblemished academic track record with 
  zero history of backlogs.
- Verification: Academic records are verified via the Controller of Examinations. 
  Misrepresentation leads to immediate debarment.

2.3 Attendance Requirements:
- Placement Readiness Training (PRT) Attendance: Mandatory minimum 85% attendance across 
  all placement preparation modules, including technical bootcamps and aptitude tests.
- General Academic Attendance: Students must satisfy the college mandate of at least 75% 
  regular academic attendance to be eligible for placement drive clearance.
- Pre-Placement Talks (PPT): Attendance is mandatory for every registered candidate for 
  that specific drive. Absence from a PPT without prior approved leave results in 
  disqualification from that company's drive.

SECTION 3: COMPANY CATEGORIZATION AND OFFER REGULATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Companies visiting the institution are classified into distinct tiers based on 
Cost to Company (CTC) packages:

3.1 Tier Classification:
- Tier 1 - Super Dream Offer: CTC of [TIER1_SUPER_DREAM_THRESHOLD: INR 12.0 LPA and above].
- Tier 1 - Dream Offer: CTC between [TIER1_DREAM_RANGE: INR 6.5 LPA to INR 11.99 LPA].
- Tier 2 - Core / Regular Placement: CTC up to [TIER2_REGULAR_CEILING: below INR 6.5 LPA].

3.2 Multiple Offer and Dream Option Regulations:
- One Student, One Regular Offer Rule: When a student secures an offer from a Tier 2 company, 
  they are excluded from attending further Tier 2 recruitment drives.
- Upgrade Rule (Dream / Super Dream Option):
  * A student holding a Tier 2 offer is permitted to participate in Tier 1 Dream or Super Dream 
    drives until they secure one upgraded offer.
  * A student holding a Tier 1 Dream offer is eligible only for Tier 1 Super Dream drives 
    offering at least [SUPER_DREAM_DELTA: 1.5x higher CTC than current offer].
  * Once a student secures a Tier 1 Super Dream offer, they are placed in the "Fully Placed" 
    category and become ineligible for any subsequent campus drives.
- Offer Acceptance Timeline: Students issued an offer letter must submit their written 
  acceptance or rejection via the Placement Portal within [OFFER_ACCEPTANCE_DAYS: 3 working days]. 
  Failure to reply within this window is deemed an automatic acceptance.

SECTION 4: RECRUITMENT DRIVE PROCEDURES AND WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recruitment drives follow a strictly monitored 5-stage workflow:

Stage 1: Notification and Student Registration
- Drive notification published via the college Placement Portal and official email.
- Registration window: Open for a designated period (typically [REGISTRATION_WINDOW_HOURS: 24 to 48 hours]). 
  Late registrations will not be entertained under any circumstance.
- Willingness Submission: Clicking "Register" constitutes a binding commitment to participate 
  in all selection rounds.

Stage 2: Pre-Placement Talk (PPT)
- Recruiter presents company profile, job role, compensation details, service agreement/bonds, 
  and work location.
- Clarifications regarding job roles and company expectations must be asked during this session.

Stage 3: Online Assessment / Screening Rounds
- Formats: Quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, and technical domain coding.
- Environment: Proctored computer labs or supervised remote assessments.
- Zero-Tolerance Malpractice: Any form of cheating or tab switching leads to immediate permanent 
  debarment from all campus placement activities.

Stage 4: Technical Interviews
- Round Structure: One to two technical rounds assessing data structures, algorithms, 
  system architecture, core engineering subjects, final year projects, and internships.
- Preparation: Students must bring 3 updated physical copies of their verified resume and college ID.

Stage 5: HR and Managerial Interviews
- Evaluates communication skills, cultural fit, willingness to relocate, and flexibility in shifts.
- Discussion of documentation, background check requirements, and joining timelines.

Stage 6: Final Results and Debriefing
- The recruiter shares the shortlist with the Placement Officer.
- Selected candidates receive official offer communications via the Placement Office.

SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Mandatory Professional Dress Code:
- Formal attire is mandatory for all placement activities:
  * Male Candidates: Formal plain shirt, dark formal trousers, formal leather shoes, and tie.
  * Female Candidates: Formal plain salwar suit / formal shirt and trousers with formal footwear.
- College identity card must be displayed at all times.

5.2 Absence and Blacklisting Policy:
- Dropping out of a drive after clearing the assessment or PPT without an approved medical 
  emergency results in debarment for the next [DEBARMENT_DRIVE_COUNT: 3 consecutive campus drives].
- Insubordination or unprofessional behaviour during an interview leads to complete cancellation 
  of placement registration for the academic year.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 6: TRAINING AND PREPARATION GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aptitude Preparation: Focus on speed math, quantitative techniques, logical deductions, 
  and reading comprehension.
- Technical Coding: Recommended platforms include LeetCode, HackerRank, and GeeksforGeeks. 
  Key topics: Arrays, Linked Lists, Trees, Dynamic Programming, SQL, and Object-Oriented Design.
- Resume Guidelines: Maximum 1 page, reverse chronological order, validated project links, 
  and no inflated or unverified claims.
- Mock Interviews: The Placement Cell arranges peer and alumni mock sessions every semester.
